Privacy Policy

Face2Face Games | Last updated October 26, 2026

Face2Face Games is made by Nathan Haring. The app does not collect data for the developer. There are no accounts, ads, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs, and no developer server receiving gameplay data.

Information on Your Device

Player labels, favorites, recent games, and sound, haptic, and game preferences are stored on your device. Real names are not required; you can keep the default player labels. Device backups are controlled by your Apple settings.

Photos and Drawings

Photo Puzzle uses Apple's system photo picker. The app receives only the picture you select, not access to your entire photo library. Pictures are processed on your device for the puzzle. Drawings are created on your device.

When you choose a photo or draw during a shared game, that content is sent to the other player so both players can see the same game. Only share content you are comfortable showing them. It is not uploaded to a developer server.

Nearby and SharePlay

Nearby play uses Apple's local-networking services. While hosting or finding a game, a device name is visible to nearby players. Connected players exchange game actions and session content to synchronize play.

SharePlay uses Apple's FaceTime and GroupActivities services. FaceTime participants exchange game actions and shared content as part of the session. Apple operates these services under its own privacy policy. Your operating system and App Store diagnostic-sharing choices are managed by Apple and your device settings.

Children and External Links

No personal information is required to play. Developer, support, and App Store links in the app are behind a grown-up check. External websites and the App Store have their own privacy practices. A grown-up should choose what content is shared in multiplayer and help with support requests.

Support Email

If you choose to contact support, your email address, message, and any attachments are received and used to respond and troubleshoot. This is voluntary, outside gameplay, and not used for advertising. Please do not send children's personal information. Contact support to request deletion of a support conversation.

This Website

This support site has no advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies added by the developer. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process connection information under its privacy statement. Visiting another website or emailing support is separate from playing the app.

Questions or Changes

Questions about privacy or support messages can be sent to F2Fgamesapp@gmail.com. If the app's practices change, this policy will be updated with a new date.
