Privacy Policy for Face2Face Games

Last Updated: January 2026

Face2Face Games ("we," "us," or "our") develops games for children. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our young users and their families.

1. Information We Collect

Face2Face Games does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) from children. We do not require users to enter names, email addresses, or phone numbers to play our games.

2. Non-Personal & Technical Information

We may collect non-personal information such as device type, operating system, and basic usage statistics to help us improve the game experience. This data is anonymous and cannot be used to identify a specific child.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not share information with third parties for marketing purposes. If we use third-party tools (like Game Center or Google Play Services), they have their own privacy policies which we encourage parents to review.

4. COPPA Compliance

Our apps are designed to be compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
